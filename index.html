How it works

On a local stack you set the price. It is signed by a test key that ships inside this page, in the format of DIA's prices. Mainnet and Mutinynet only take prices signed by DIA.

This is real bitcoin. These contracts are new and have not been audited. An offer or a position cannot leave Arkade on its own if the operator stops, and its coin must be settled before it expires. Send only what you can afford to lose, or switch to Mutinynet to try it with test coins.

This browser is not saving anything for this page. Your key and your addresses exist only in this tab: back them up under “Your key” before you pay anything, or you will not be able to close or withdraw after a reload.

Leverage
Take profit at

Nothing is taken until you pay the address. Whatever you send becomes your margin.

Offer bitcoin as liquidity. Traders open positions against it, and you are the other side of each one: you earn the spread, a holding fee for as long as a trade stays open, and the margins of liquidated trades. You pay the profits of winning ones.

Each trade locks its maximum profit out of your offer until it ends. You can withdraw the rest at any time.
